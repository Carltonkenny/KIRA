# Why KIRA Exists

## The Problem

Every AI coding agent in 2026 — Antigravity, OpenCode, Cline, Claude Desktop — suffers from **prompt amnesia**. They evaluate each prompt in isolation. Every time you open a session, you re-explain:

- Your tech stack
- Your coding style
- Your project structure
- Your preferences
- The context from yesterday

You've been coding with AI agents for long enough to know: **80% of your time is spent re-explaining context, not writing code.**

## The Deeper Problem

Existing prompt tools don't solve this. They fall into two broken categories:

### Category 1: Prompt Optimizers (Stateless)
Tools like MCP Prompt Optimizer, Simple Prompt Optimizer, Gemini Prompt Enhancer. They take a prompt in, spit a "better" prompt out. No memory. No learning. No personalization. Every call starts from zero.

**What they lack:** They don't know YOU. They optimize for generic "good prompts," not for YOUR workflow, YOUR agents, YOUR patterns.

### Category 2: Memory Frameworks (Generic)
Tools like Mem0, Letta, Zep. They provide persistent memory for agents. But they're infrastructure layers — they don't know what to DO with the memory for prompt optimization.

**What they lack:** They store memories but don't use them to make your prompts better. They're plumbing, not intelligence.

## The Gap

Nobody has built a **personal, self-learning prompt middleware** that:

1. **Remembers** your preferences, stack, and patterns (like Mem0)
2. **Optimizes** your prompts using that memory (like prompt optimizers)
3. **Learns** from outcomes — which prompts worked, which didn't (like nobody)
4. **Adapts** without being explicitly taught — it watches your behavior (like nobody)
5. **Translates** your sloppy human shorthand into clean agent instructions (like nobody)
6. **Knows when to shut up** — doesn't over-engineer simple commands (like nobody)

## Why KIRA

KIRA fills this exact gap. It's the layer between your brain and your agents that:

- **Learns your language** — "do the thing" becomes a concrete, context-aware instruction
- **Gets better over time** — without you teaching it anything
- **Works with any MCP agent** — drop-in MCP server, universal compatibility
- **Respects your flow** — enhances complex prompts, passes through simple ones
- **Builds your fingerprint** — knows your patterns better than you do

It's the AI that talks to AI, on your behalf, in the language AI understands, using everything it's learned about you.
